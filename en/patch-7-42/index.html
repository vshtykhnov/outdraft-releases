Dota 2 patch 7.42: what matters for drafts and item builds

A patch like 7.42 arrives as hundreds of small edits to items, heroes, neutrals and the map. Reading them takes an evening, and the question that matters most, how they add up inside a draft, is not something the notes can answer. This page keeps only the changes that touch picks and item builds. What actually works is decided by real matches, and that is where Outdraft looks: it already works on 7.42, and its picks, counterpicks and item builds account for the new patch.

Updated: 20 Nov 20268 min readSource: official 7.42 patch notes, 18 Nov 2026

Patch 7.42 at a glance

Valve released gameplay update 7.42 on 18 November 2026 [1]. It changes every one of the 127 heroes, rebuilds a good part of the shop and reshapes neutral items. For the draft and the item screen, these are the numbers to keep in mind:

6new shop items, plus the returning Quarterstaff
3shop items removed: Khanda, Javelin, Helm of the Overlord
3hero abilities removed: Swiftslash, MegaMeepo, Drums of Slom
5 → 4neutral item tiers
13neutral artifacts cycled out
120 → 200base hero health

Six new items: recipes and cost

All six come from items you already know, so they slot into existing build paths rather than replace them outright [1][2].

Read on paper, Ristul’s Devotion and Caduceus are team items: one is an aura for a right-click lineup, the other turns the Phylactery path into something that also helps allies. Skypiercer and Sabatons are late, expensive answers for melee cores. Bloodslinger is a cheap early piece for Intelligence heroes and supports. Who should actually buy them, and when, is a question for real matches rather than for the patch notes.

Quarterstaff returns; Khanda, Javelin and Helm of the Overlord are gone

  • Quarterstaff is back: 900 gold, +10 damage and +10 attack speed. It now builds into Butterfly (5000, was 5450; replaces Claymore), Monkey King Bar (replaces Javelin) and Mask of Madness (1800, was 1900) [1].
  • Javelin is removed. Maelstrom takes a 900-gold recipe instead and still costs 2950. Bloodthorn no longer needs Javelin, costs 6250 and loses its Pierce passive. Monkey King Bar no longer gives melee heroes +50 attack range.
  • Khanda and Helm of the Overlord are removed from the game.
  • Recipes that change build paths: Heaven’s Halberd is now Ogre Axe + Helm of Iron Will (2400, was 3300), gives +12 Strength and no evasion. Abyssal Blade and Harpoon now build from Dragon Lance, and Dragon Lance gives melee heroes +25 attack range. Crimson Guard includes a Buckler and its aura. Sange, Yasha and Kaya cost 2000 each, so their combinations cost 4000.
For builds

Builds saved from 7.41 can quietly break: any path through Javelin, Khanda or the old Butterfly, Abyssal Blade, Harpoon and Heaven’s Halberd recipes no longer exists in that form.

Less spell power, more Item Potency

Two system changes reach almost every build [1]:

  • Spell Power Reduction. Items now add less to ability scaling (spell amplification, area of effect, cast range), and most magic and pure damage spells deal less. Examples: Kaya amplification 10% → 6%, Aether Lens cast range +225 → +200, Octarine Core cooldown reduction 25% → 20%, Shiva’s Guard slow 40% → 30%.
  • Item Potency. On-hit effects such as Desolator’s armor reduction, Eye of Skadi’s slow or Skull Basher’s bash chance now scale with the attack’s potency, 100% by default. Illusions have 20% potency and can now apply most on-hit effects (Skull Basher is one of the exceptions); Manta Style illusions have 35%, Illusion rune ones 40%.

On paper, this takes some value away from magic burst built on amplification items and gives illusion heroes a new reason to buy on-hit items. How large either effect is in practice cannot be read from the notes.

Neutral items: four tiers and pools known in advance

  • Tiers: 0:00, 15:00, 30:00 and 50:00; infinite Madstone from 65:00 [1].
  • Pools: each tier is split into pools, one artifact is picked from each, and all options are fixed at the start of the game. You can see them in the Neutrals tab of the shop, which makes it easier to plan item timings.
  • New: Red Ledger (tier 1), Brick (tier 2), Balmhook and Vile Phial (tier 3), Helm of the Hivemind (tier 4). Returning: Imp Claw and Safety Bubble (tier 2), Ceremonial Robe (tier 3), Flicker, Giant’s Ring and Mind Breaker (tier 4). Revenant’s Brooch is reworked into a tier 3 artifact.
  • Cycled out (13): Dagger of Ristul, Duelist Gloves, Occult Bracelet, Essence Ring, Medallion of Courage, Poor Man’s Shield, Searing Signet, Cloak of Flames, Flayer’s Bota, Giant’s Maul, Unrelenting Eye, Spider Legs and Stygian Desolator.

For these heroes, matchup numbers from 7.41 describe a hero that no longer exists, so old counterpicks deserve a fresh look [1][3].

Global changes that shift the draft

  • Sturdier heroes: base health 120 → 200; health per point of Strength 22 → 20; magic resistance per Intelligence 0.1% → 0.125%.
  • Experience: every hero passively gets 50 experience per minute after the horn, even while dead; level 30 now takes 66,600 experience instead of 64,400.
  • Gold: passive gold stays at 90 per minute instead of growing to 111 by 60:00; hero kill bounty changes from 125 + 8 × level to 100 + 10 × level; towers and Roshan give less.
  • Tormentor: first spawn at 15:00 instead of 20:00. Each kill drops the new Greater Horn of Battle, which summons a mega creep wave in every lane; from the second kill it also drops Aghanim’s Shard.
  • Roshan: the respawn timer shrinks by one minute after each kill, down to 5 minutes.
  • Glyph: 2 charges with a 6-minute replenish, no longer reset by losing buildings.
  • Vision and ganks: Smoke of Deceit holds 4 charges and restocks in 330 s; the Invisibility rune also grants a disguise against True Sight away from enemies.

What this means for picks, counterpicks and builds

Each point below is a direction, not a verdict. Hundreds of changes interact with each other, and only matches show the result.

  • Early fights carry more margin. With 80 more base health, level 1–3 kill combos have more room to fall short. How much this reshapes lanes is a question for the data.
  • Magic versus physical. Lower spell power and Item Potency on illusions both point the same way: a little less for burst-magic lineups, a little more for right-click and illusion carries.
  • Habit builds break first. Without Javelin and Khanda, and with new recipes for Butterfly, MKB, Abyssal Blade, Harpoon and Halberd, a familiar build may simply be impossible to assemble.
  • Reworked heroes need new counters. Juggernaut, Meepo, Beastmaster, Lycan, Chaos Knight and Nyx Assassin play differently enough that last patch’s matchups say little.
  • Objectives come earlier. Tormentor at 15:00 with a mega creep wave and a Shard, plus a shrinking Roshan timer, favours lineups that can take objectives early.
