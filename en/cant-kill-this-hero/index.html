Counter-picking

Why can’t I kill this hero?

When a hero feels “unkillable”, the problem is almost never the hero. It is that your team has no tool against it: nothing to cut healing, break a passive or hit an area. The numbers back this up: the right answer drops Huskar’s win rate from 45.8% to 38.7%. The easiest place to fix it is hero selection.

Updated: 19 Oct 20265 min readLive numbers: Dotabuff / OpenDota / D2PT, 19 Oct 2026, patch 7.41

Why “unfair” is usually the draft

When nothing works, it is easiest to blame the system. In the CHI 2018 study on losing streaks, a third of players (33%) blamed how matchmaking works [3]. But the feeling of “I can’t kill him” usually has a very concrete cause: your lineup has no mechanic that works against that hero.

It shows more at lower ranks. The HSE study of 5 million matches found that at normal skill the result depends more on picks than at very high skill [4]. Strong players know how to play into counters, while most of us are confident on a few heroes and can’t always adapt.

Mechanics that shut down “unkillable” heroes

Almost every “unkillable” hero relies on one strength. Turn it off and the hero becomes normal.

A good check during the draft: name how your team will kill the enemy’s main hero. If there is no answer, take it with the last pick or plan it in your items.

Breakdown: Huskar

Huskar gets stronger the lower his health is, and relies on his passive, Berserker’s Blood, and on healing. His overall win rate is 45.76%, and his worst matchups on Dotabuff look like this [1]:

38.70%vs Ancient Apparition (28,846 matches)
41.16%vs Viper (48,510 matches)
38.68%vs Wraith King

The logic is clear: Ancient Apparition stops healing, Viper breaks the passive. Even in his best matchup, vs Anti-Mage, Huskar wins only 51.06% [1]. He only becomes “OP” when the enemy has no answer.

Breakdown: Meepo

Meepo is a strong hero overall (52.95%), but he has very specific weaknesses [2]. Against Earthshaker his win rate drops to 44.88% over 28,458 matches. His Dotabuff Disadvantage is 8.38% vs Earthshaker, 8.40% vs Elder Titan, 8.04% vs Winter Wyvern and 8.00% vs Underlord.

What they have in common: area damage and control that hits all clones at once, and in Winter Wyvern’s case a curse that makes the Meepos attack each other. Meanwhile, for most of his other matchups Meepo’s Disadvantage is under 3% [2]. So “countering Meepo” doesn’t mean any hero with a stun. It means a few specific ones.

“Can’t kill” = “didn’t answer in the draft”

In most drafts the enemy has one or two heroes the game depends on. If you have no answer to them, the problem keeps growing. Live prediction studies show that by minute 5 the result can be predicted up to 85% of the time [6], so an early edge snowballs fast. An answer that shows up at minute 25 is often too late.

That is why the best moment to deal with a threat is hero selection. And it is better to answer the main threat than to try to counter everyone. Models that look at specific hero pairs predict results much better than overall win rates [5].

No answer in the draft? Items

  • Against healing and regen: Spirit Vessel, Eye of Skadi.
  • Against passives: Silver Edge.
  • Against saving spells: Orchid Malevolence, Bloodthorn.
  • Against magic damage and control: Black King Bar.

Items are not as strong as the right hero, because you have to farm them first. But they beat hoping the enemy makes a mistake.

A quick check during the draft

Before the last picks, ask your team five questions. If any answer is “no”, that is your future “unkillable” hero.

  1. How do we cut healing if the enemy has regen or heals?
  2. Do we have break against a strong passive?
  3. What hits an area if the enemy has clones or illusions?
  4. Do we have a silence for a hero that saves itself with spells?
  5. Is all our damage one type, only physical or only magic?

Questions

How do you kill Huskar in Dota 2?

Turn off what he relies on: healing and his passive. On Dotabuff, Huskar does worst against Ancient Apparition (38.7%), Wraith King (38.7%) and Viper (41.2%). Spirit Vessel, Eye of Skadi and Silver Edge help as items.

Who counters Meepo?

On Dotabuff the strongest counters are Earthshaker (Meepo wins 44.9%), Elder Titan, Winter Wyvern and Underlord. They all have area damage and control. Against most other heroes Meepo loses very little.

Why can’t I kill anyone at my rank?

At normal ranks the draft affects the result more: players are confident on fewer heroes and adapt less. If your lineup has no healing reduction, break or area damage, some heroes will feel unkillable.

What if it’s too late to counter pick?

Answer the threat with items (Spirit Vessel, Silver Edge, Orchid, BKB) and avoid fighting where the enemy’s main hero is strongest. Next time, solve it in the draft.
