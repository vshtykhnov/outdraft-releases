Can I get banned for using it?

No. Outdraft never reads or changes the game's memory or files. It only looks at the picks on screen and analyzes them, and gets your hero and items through Valve's official Game State Integration. It works the same way as Dota Plus hero suggestions, Overwolf overlays and the GSI lighting in SteelSeries and Logitech apps.

How do I use it?

Run Outdraft, then start Dota 2 in borderless window so the app can see the picks. Find a match: picks are recognized automatically and suggestions appear right away. Each hero shows its impact as a percentage and a lane rating (Good, Even, Hard, or Very Hard). The most influential hero has a star, the weakest has a down arrow, and the overall draft advantage is shown as win rate (e.g. "Dire 64%"). That's it, no logins, passwords or permissions.
