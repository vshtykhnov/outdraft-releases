Can I get banned for using it?

No. Outdraft never reads or changes the game's memory or files. It only looks at the picks on screen and analyzes them, and gets your hero and items through Valve's official Game State Integration. It works the same way as Dota Plus hero suggestions, Overwolf overlays and the GSI lighting in SteelSeries and Logitech apps.

How do I use it?

Run Outdraft, then Dota 2 in borderless window. During hero selection it recognizes each pick and recalculates recommendations, win chance, lanes, and timings. When the match starts it switches to items, skills, and Live, where your pace is compared with the usual values for that hero and role. No account is required; linking Steam is optional and only personalizes your pool and bans.
